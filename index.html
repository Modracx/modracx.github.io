The passage

Every build walks the same four stretches.

The order matters. Skipping the first one is why redesigns get rebuilt.

Stretch one

Understand the constraint

What the business actually runs on, what the team can maintain, and what the current system gets wrong. Before a line of code.

Stretch two

Build the spine

Data model, key flows, and the interface that carries them. Made to be read by whoever inherits it.

Stretch three

Harden and tune

Performance, technical SEO, access control, and patching. Treated as build quality, not as a phase-two invoice.

Stretch four

Hand over well

Documentation, editing setups people can use, and support if the work needs continuity after launch.
