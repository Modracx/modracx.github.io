# MODRACX Portfolio Design System

## Core Pattern

- Digital workshop portfolio
- Room-based navigation
- Terminal-style command surface
- Dashboard status blocks
- Outcome-first content

## Token Intent

- Primitive: dark graphite base with brass and teal accents
- Semantic: surface, elevated, accent, muted, border, destructive
- Component: cards, pills, nav, buttons, terminal windows, status panels

## Typography

- Display: Space Grotesk
- Body: Inter
- Mono: JetBrains Mono

## Layout

- Use a strong hero with clear CTA hierarchy
- Mix visual rooms with content cards
- Keep sections scannable and modular

## Interaction

- Visible focus states
- 44px touch targets
- Smooth but restrained motion
- No hover-only critical interactions

## Content Strategy

- Explain how systems work
- Show what problems are solved
- Describe decision-making, not just output
- Center business outcomes and technical depth
