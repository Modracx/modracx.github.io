Orqa vs Firefly III, Actual Budget & YNAB: The Best Self-Hosted Personal Finance App

Looking for a privacy-focused self-hosted personal finance platform or an open-source Firefly III / YNAB alternative? Compare ledger integrity, multi-currency support, and modern UI engineering.

By Kenneth D'Silva • Reading Time: 15 min read • Category: Architecture & Cloud

Why Self-Hosted Personal Finance Matters in 2026

Commercial SaaS budgeting tools like YNAB, Monarch Money, and Copilot come with increasing subscription fees and require handing over your entire financial history to third-party cloud aggregators. On the other hand, traditional self-hosted solutions often feel complex or dated.

Orqa combines the cryptographic precision of an immutable double-entry bookkeeping ledger with modern Next.js 15, PostgreSQL schema design, and zero-based budgeting workflows.

Key Capabilities of Orqa

1. True Zero-Based Envelope Budgeting

Assign every dollar a job before the month starts. Unallocated funds remain transparent, preventing phantom savings and overdraft discrepancies.

2. Immutable Transaction Ledger

Financial records cannot be silently mutated. Corrections generate explicit reversal journal entries, ensuring forensic-grade accounting accuracy.
