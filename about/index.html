How I work

Three principles that decide most of the arguments.

Goal-aligned thinking

Every decision traces back to a business goal, a user need, or an operational reality. Cosmetic work without a purpose is not the point.

Security first

Patching, updates, access control, and production safety are core responsibilities rather than optional line items.

Performance maturity

Fast loads, crawlability, and search structure are built in from day one, because retrofitting them costs more than doing them.

Questions

Quick answers for hiring teams and clients.

Do you only work on Magento and Shopify?

No. They are the strongest current specialisations, but I also work across CMS platforms, custom sites, ERP-style systems, internal tools, and broader frontend and backend stacks.

Can you handle both design and development thinking?

Yes. I work comfortably where brand presentation, UX decisions, frontend interaction, and implementation detail all have to stay aligned.

Do you consider SEO and security during the build?

Yes. Technical SEO and platform safety are part of build quality here, not afterthought add-ons.

What does your Magento 2 and Shopify experience actually cover?

Storefront and theme delivery across Hyva, Luma, Breeze, and Liquid; custom extensions, app logic, and workflow rules; checkout and conversion path work; GraphQL and REST integrations, including payment and shipping providers; and the unglamorous side — patching, performance tuning, and debugging production incidents on stores that are already trading.

Do you work on infrastructure and deployment as well as application code?

Yes. CI/CD pipelines in GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins; containerisation with Docker and orchestration on Kubernetes; infrastructure as code with Terraform, CloudFormation, or Pulumi; monitoring and log aggregation; and provisioning on AWS, GCP, or Azure. Delivery that cannot be repeated reliably is not finished delivery.

How do you work with an existing team?

As a contributor rather than a parallel track. That means reading the codebase before proposing to replace it, matching existing conventions where they are sound, arguing for changes where they are not, and writing documentation and handover material as part of the work instead of at the end of it.

Where are you based and how do you engage?

Ahmedabad, India, working in English with clients worldwide. Engagements run as freelance projects, contract builds, retained support, or collaboration inside an existing team.
